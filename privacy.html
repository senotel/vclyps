Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Your video never uploads. The AI reads your words and a few still frames to make its choices and, with your permission, how you edit helps train vClyps' own AI.

This page explains exactly what that means. vClyps is made by Vevris ("we"). It covers the vClyps website and the vClyps app in app stores, which are the same editor.

What stays on your device

vClyps is built so that the heavy work happens on your phone or computer, not on our servers.

  • Your footage, photos and audio. They are opened, played, cut and exported on your device. The finished video is made on your device and saved there. We never receive a copy.
  • Captions. Speech is turned into text by a model that runs on your device.
  • Finding clips in a long video. Listening for laughter and reactions and watching for faces and scene changes all happen on your device.
  • Your project. Your timeline, settings and edit history are kept in your browser's storage on your device so you can pick up where you left off. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app deletes them.

What leaves your device, and why

Some features need help from a service on the internet. Here is each one, what is sent and who receives it.

The AI Director (when you press Edit or Find clips)

To decide how to cut your video, the AI Director is sent the words that were said (the transcript, with timings), a few still frames from your footage, your written instructions and basic facts such as each clip's length. It is never sent the video or its sound.

This goes through our own relay (a Cloudflare Worker run by Vevris), which holds the AI keys and passes the request to an AI provider: Google (Gemini), and as a backup Groq or Mistral. The relay does not store what passes through it. The AI providers process it under their own terms and may keep it for a limited time for safety and abuse checks.

Translated captions

If you ask for captions in another language, the caption text is sent through the same relay to an AI provider to be translated. Nothing else is sent.

Searches for music, sound effects, GIFs and photos

When you search, or when the AI Director looks for a track or a cutaway, the search words are sent to the library that holds them: Openverse and Freesound for music and sound effects, and Klipy and Pexels (through our relay) for GIFs, photos and video clips. The file you pick is then downloaded to your device.

Importing from other services

If you choose to import from Google Drive, Google Photos, Dropbox, Zoom or Vimeo, you sign in with that service and it asks for your permission. vClyps only reads the files you pick. Zoom and Vimeo downloads pass through our relay, which forwards them to your device without keeping them. We do not keep your sign-in for those services after the import.

Things your device downloads

The speech and vision models, fonts and some program code are downloaded the first time a feature needs them, from public delivery networks (jsDelivr, Google Cloud Storage, Google Fonts and Fontshare). Like any website, those services see your device's internet address when it downloads from them.

Google MediaPipe usage reports

The tools that listen for laughter and look for faces while finding clips are Google's MediaPipe. While they are running, MediaPipe sends Google anonymous reports about how the tools performed. These reports do not contain your video, your sound or any picture from it.

Training vClyps' own AI

vClyps is building its own editing AI, Senotel Vision. It learns from how people edit: which cuts were kept, what was changed after the AI's edit, which music and sounds were chosen. It never learns from your footage.

Your edit history is kept on your device today and is not sent to us. If we ask to use it for training, it will only be with your clear permission, which you can say no to or take back at any time, and it will never include your video, your sound or your still frames.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies.
  • We do not need an account to use vClyps, so we do not hold your name or email.

Your choices and rights

  • Not using a feature keeps its data on your device. You can edit by hand, make captions and export without the AI Director ever being called.
  • Deleting your data. Everything vClyps keeps is on your device. Clear your browser's site data for vClyps, or uninstall the app, and it is gone.
  • Your rights. Depending on where you live (for example under Thailand's PDPA or the EU's GDPR) you may have the right to ask what we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to how it is used. Because we keep almost nothing, the answer is usually short, but you can always ask.

Children

vClyps is for people aged 13 and over. If you are under the age at which you can agree to this on your own where you live, please use vClyps with a parent or guardian.

Changes

If we change how vClyps handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change means sending something new off your device, the app will tell you before it happens.
